# Behaviors - sp-today.com

## Header
- **Scroll:** Header transitions from transparent bg to opaque (#0f172a/90 + backdrop-blur) on scroll
- **Mobile menu:** Hamburger toggle expands/collapses nav with max-h transition
- **Hover:** Nav items change text color (#94a3b8 → #fff) with bg highlight

## Hero Section
- **City selector:** Dropdown to switch between "دمشق - عام" and "الحسكة" cities

## Currency Table
- **Hover:** Row bg changes on hover (#334155/30)

## News Items
- **Hover:** Card bg changes, image scales up slightly

## Footer Social Icons
- **Hover:** Icons change to brand colors (Facebook blue, WhatsApp green, Telegram blue)
